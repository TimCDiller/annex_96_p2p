Role cards

Each student gets a numbered card: a household with energy to buy or sell and private information about what that energy is worth to them. The answer key shows what happens when everyone bids exactly those values. Change the deck code for a fresh set of numbers. The form deals the same cards when the number of students and the deck code match CARDS and DECK_CODE in apps_script.gs (10 and "1" unless you changed them).

Answer key

The benchmark: every card bids exactly its values (buys at what the energy is worth, sells at the least the household would accept). Students who shade their bids may do better or worse; that is the discussion. The score is what a household gains from the market compared with not taking part.
