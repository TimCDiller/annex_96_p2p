Market clearing explorer

Eight participants, up to five bids each, one hour of the course market. Change any bid, the amount or the price, and the market clears again: the figure shows every order over the prices where it counts, how much would trade at each price, and what each participant ends up with. Each tab starts from a different market. Reset puts the tab back to its starting bids.

How the market clears

  1. Net positions. Each participant's bids are reduced to its net position at every price: a buyer or a seller there, never both. A buy counts at its price and every lower price, a sell at its price and every higher price.
  2. Most volume. Of all prices from 0¢ to the grid price, the market picks the ones that trade the most energy (demand and supply at that price, whichever is smaller).
  3. Closest to the crossing. If several prices trade that most, it takes those where demand and supply are closest, and the middle of them if that still leaves several.
  4. One price for everyone. Everyone in the money trades at that price, whatever they bid. The short side is filled completely; the long side shares what there is in proportion to what each bid.
